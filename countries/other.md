# Countries without a module

Ask where the person lives and which other countries have relevant identity, tax or financial ties, without collecting identifiers. Load every applicable module, deduplicate overlapping steps and research only the next useful local action. A missing module does not block country-neutral email and recovery help.

Use current government/regulator or provider sources. Verify the source's authority beyond its domain suffix; follow official links when services use another host. Check publication dates and superseding rules. Record URL, checked date, supported claim and uncertainty in the private note if needed; never publish personal findings. If a page is inaccessible, a claim is disputed or official guidance is missing, label it unverified and give an official support route. Do not invent an equivalent protection.

| Need | What to verify before recommending it |
| --- | --- |
| Credit/identity | Official credit records, access/correction rights and whether a freeze or fraud alert exists; scope, cost and recovery. A debt record is not real-time fraud detection. |
| Lost ID | Current replacement/reporting route and jurisdiction; do not assume an old lost-ID register still operates. |
| Tax/digital government | Supported login protection, recovery and delegated-access review. Do not infer tax duties or change representatives. |
| Phone | Carrier SIM/number-transfer protection, service-loss recovery and lost-phone blocking. Separate these from device SIM PIN and remote erasure. |
| Money fraud | Bank/payment provider's urgent fraud contact first, then financial complaint escalation and crime reporting; confirm any waiting period applies to escalation. |

For each selected step, show the official destination, why, who acts, safe steps, non-secret verification and a stopping point. The user handles identity documents and submissions privately. Optional marketing registries and less relevant enrollments can wait. Cadence is agreed with the person; do not convert a free-report entitlement into a requirement to submit documents repeatedly.
