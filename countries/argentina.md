# Argentina

Load for people living in Argentina or with Argentine identity or financial ties. Combine with [US abroad](us-person-abroad.md) when relevant. Use the core skill's one-action format and private evidence rules. The person enters DNI/CUIL/CUIT, número de trámite, Clave Fiscal, tokens and codes directly in official services, never here. [Sources and verification limits](../guides/sources.md) apply.

## Money and credit records

Start with the bank/wallet if money is at risk. For routine checking, use the [BCRA Central de Deudores](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/situacion-crediticia/) privately. It is a credit/debt record, not a real-time transaction alert or freeze. An unfamiliar entry may be an error or fraud: investigate with the reporting institution first, following [BCRA correction guidance](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/corregir-datos-desactualizados-en-la-central-de-deudores/). Do not declare identity theft from one entry or promise that an empty record means no fraud.

For private credit reports, [AAIP explains access rights](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/derechos), including free access every six months and a 10-calendar-day response period. Use the bureau's official access-rights route and current requirements; no agent handling of identity copies. This module has not established a universal US-style freeze mechanism for Argentina. Do not claim none can exist or sell monitoring as an equivalent. Research provider-specific protections if needed.

## Lost DNI

Do **not** direct people to DOCUEST / Registro Nacional de Documentos de Identidad Cuestionados. [AAIP Resolución 180/2025](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/resoluci%C3%B3n-180-2025-418054/texto), published September 30, 2025, repealed its founding and portal rules. Older official pages can remain reachable. Do not repeat their lender-check obligation as current guidance.

Use the current [RENAPER replacement-DNI service](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/tramitar-un-nuevo-ejemplar-de-dni) for requirements and booking; confirm the person's current location and age before advising the route. Ask local police/fiscalía for the relevant theft-report process when needed. Do not infer a new reporting requirement from the repeal.

## Government logins

For ARCA Clave Fiscal, use [official Clave Fiscal help](https://www.afip.gob.ar/clavefiscal/) and verify the current ARCA/AFIP-linked destination. Offer a unique password, review of delegated access, and supported second-factor/Token ARCA options with recovery planning. Confirm current activation requirements before a trip, ATM action or security-level change. Delegated access can be legitimate; do not remove an accountant without checking.

For [Mi Argentina](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/miargentina/preguntasfrecuentes), check current security, recovery and session options in the official service. Do not assert that an authenticator option is absent based on an old FAQ. Validating identity or a phone number is not automatically proof that two-step login is enabled.

## Phone and messaging

Ask the carrier about account, SIM-change and number-transfer protections for this line. Consult current [ENACOM guidance](https://www.enacom.gob.ar/portabilidad/) when needed; do not promise that identity checks prevent all takeovers. Never share an unexpected transfer code. A device SIM PIN is separate and must never be guessed.

For a stolen/lost phone, [ENACOM's *910 route](https://www.enacom.gob.ar/denuncia-al-910_p4034) covers blocking the line and equipment on mobile networks. Verify current availability and use the carrier's official alternative when necessary. This does not erase the phone, disable Wi-Fi or replace lost-device mode. Follow [incident guidance](../guides/incidents.md) for safe locking and financial containment; do not automatically wipe or remove the only recovery factor.

For WhatsApp, use official account security/recovery guidance linked from [UFECI's takeover warning](https://www.fiscales.gob.ar/ciberdelincuencia/consejos-de-la-ufeci-para-evitar-maniobras-que-toman-el-control-de-las-cuentas-de-whatsapp/). Review linked devices and two-step verification privately. If taken over, follow current official recovery instructions rather than automatically reinstalling. Warn contacts through a known alternate channel only with authorization; do not promise immediate recovery.

## Reporting and follow-up

1. Contact the bank/wallet immediately through a known official route. Ask about containment and keep the claim number privately. Do not automatically close all cards or accounts.
2. For crime reporting, consult local police/fiscalía or [UFECI](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/ufeci/). Verify current contact details there before submitting personal material. UFECI lists `denunciasufeci@mpf.gov.ar`; do not “correct” it based on the domain suffix alone.
3. For an unresolved financial complaint, [BCRA's fraud-complaint process](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/realizar-un-reclamo-ante-el-banco-central-por-fraude-o-estafa/) requires first complaining to the institution and at least 10 business days before escalation. This waiting period applies to escalation, not the initial urgent fraud call. Confirm eligibility and current documentation requirements on the official page; the user submits them privately.

Bank/wallet alert, recovery and token options vary. Load current provider guidance only for the institution in the current step; do not assume one bank's steps apply to another. Optional advertising-call enrollment is separate from account security and cannot stop scammers. Choose record/session reviews with the user using [the recurring guide](../guides/recurring.md); do not prescribe monthly document submissions.
