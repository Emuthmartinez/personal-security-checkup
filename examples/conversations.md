# Synthetic conversations

All people, progress and outcomes below are fictional. These illustrate response style, not actual audits or delivered reminders. Do not copy real account details into examples.

## First use: ten minutes, easily overwhelmed

**Person:** I keep hearing about hacks. I have ten minutes and don't know where to start.

**Agent:** Is something happening now, such as money you didn't send, a missing phone or losing account access? Or is this a routine check?

**Person:** Routine. I live in Argentina and also have a US SSN.

**Agent:** I'll use both country guides when we reach those steps. For today, can you still sign in to your main email? You don't need to give me its address.

**Person:** Yes, it's Google.

**Agent:**

> **Next:** check your email recovery options, about three minutes.
> **Why:** this email may be how you get back into other accounts.
> **I can:** explain [Google's official account guidance](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825) and help if a label differs.
> **You do:** open your Google Account directly, go to its security settings, and privately check whether the recovery phone/email shown are yours and still accessible. Don't paste their values here.
> **Check:** tell me only “I can use them,” “one is old,” or “not sure.”
> **Stop here:** don't change anything if you can't use the listed recovery options; we can take the official recovery route instead.

**Person:** I can use them. That's enough today.

**Agent:** Done: recovery access for main email, confirmed by you today. Needs you: choose a time to check two-step login. Not checked: the rest. Resume with “Continue at main email two-step login.” Would you like a minimal resume note to copy into your own private note?

## Blocked midway

**Person:** I turned two-step login on. Should I delete my old number? I can't find my backup codes.

**Agent:** Done: two-step login turned on, reported by you; I haven't verified recovery or a new sign-in. Needs you: find or regenerate recovery material privately using the provider's official instructions. Keep your working session and old method for now. Not checked: replacement sign-in and recovery. We can stop here and resume with “Test the replacement sign-in after recovery is ready.”

**Person:** Can I send you a screenshot of the code page?

**Agent:** Keep codes, enrollment QR codes and screenshots private. You can tell me only whether the recovery material is stored somewhere you can reach if this device is lost. I'll pause while you handle it.

## Urgent takeover during a routine session

**Person:** My phone was stolen. It is my only login-code device, and my bank just showed a transfer I didn't make.

**Agent:**

> **Next:** contact your bank immediately from another trusted phone or device.
> **Why:** it may be able to contain the fraud or stop a payment; recovery isn't guaranteed.
> **I can:** help find the bank's official fraud contact from its own site if you name the bank, without account details.
> **You do:** use its official app/site or the number on your card, report the unauthorized transfer and stolen phone, and ask how to contain access while preserving recovery.
> **Check:** tell me only whether you reached the bank and have its next instruction. Keep any claim number private.
> **Stop here:** don't send codes or transfer money to a “safe account.”

**Person:** I called. They blocked access and gave me instructions.

**Agent:** That containment is reported by you; we haven't verified recovery. Next, from a trusted device use the phone maker's lost-device service to mark it lost, following its official instructions. I can guide you once we know whether it is iPhone or Android. Don't erase it or remove it from your account while it is your only recovery device. We'll contact the carrier next using an official route.

## A routine recurring review

**Person:** It's reminder day. Nothing seems wrong. Did you check everything?

**Agent:** The reminder doesn't inspect accounts. Our note says you checked email recovery last month; I have no new account evidence. Have you changed your phone/number or had any unexpected alerts since then?

**Person:** No. My bank only supports text codes. Its alerts look normal to me.

**Agent:** Keep text-code login on; it adds protection. Done: bank alerts reviewed today, reported by you. Needs you: one small check of pending phone updates in the official settings. Not checked: current email sessions and backup restoration. I won't call these unchanged without a current check. Would you like the update steps now?

**Person:** Later. Can you schedule the next check?

**Agent:** I can't access a scheduler here, so nothing is installed. You can add “Personal check-in” in your calendar at a monthly time you choose, check its saved timezone and next event, and try a harmless notification test. Resume with “One short personal security check-in.” You can also use that prompt whenever you're ready.

If a scheduler is available, the agent follows [setup and readback tests](../guides/recurring.md) instead. It never claims delivery from a create request alone.
