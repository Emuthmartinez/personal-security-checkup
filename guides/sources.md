# Official sources and limits

Existing account-security source review: 2026-10-24. Identity-protection and everyday-security sources below were checked on 2026-10-31. These sources support the specific claims below; this is not a blanket certification of every menu, individual eligibility rule or country's protections. Recheck the official source at the time of action and check for superseding rules. No personal account or identifier was queried. Cadences, state transitions and response formats are product design choices, not official universal requirements.

| Source | Supported scope / limit |
| --- | --- |
| [Google compromised account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825) | Recovery, activity/devices, recovery details, connected access, forwarding and filters. Follow the user's provider's process rather than applying Google menus to other services. |
| [Google passkeys](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13548313?hl=en) | Adding a passkey leaves existing authentication/recovery factors in place. It does not eliminate phone-number dependencies. Google warns against creating a passkey on a shared device: someone able to unlock it can access the account, even after sign-out. |
| [FTC two-factor guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/use-two-factor-authentication-protect-your-accounts) | Two-factor login adds protection; available methods differ. Retain SMS when it is the only supported option. |
| [NIST SP 800-63B-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html) | Section 3.1.1.2 rejects arbitrary periodic password changes and calls for changes on evidence of compromise. This is a verifier standard, not a personal monthly-audit mandate. |
| [Apple lost device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101593) and [Google lost Android](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6160491) | Remote finding, securing and erasing are distinct and depend on setup/device state. Follow current official recovery instructions before erasure. |
| [Apple SIM PIN](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118228) | Locks SIM/eSIM use on restart/removal. Never guess PIN/PUK. This is distinct from carrier-side number-transfer protection. |
| [Apple recovery key](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109345) | Optional key disables standard account recovery. Apple says not to store it in Apple Passwords, iCloud Photos, Notes or Drive; lost account access can block those stores. Not a default recommendation for everyone. |
| [FTC scam response](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) | Contact the payment provider promptly and ask about recovery; results vary. Reporting does not guarantee a refund. |
| [HIBP FAQ](https://haveibeenpwned.com/Faqs) | A lookup concerns recorded breaches, not comprehensive assurance. It submits an identifier to an independent service; get consent or use existing provider warnings privately. No identifiers were submitted during this review. |
| [RFC 5545](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5545#section-3.3.10) | Monthly recurrence syntax and start/timezone semantics; actual scheduler support, notification delivery and DST behavior require provider readback/tests. |
| [Apple Time Machine restore](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/restore-items-backed-up-with-time-machine-mh11422/mac) | File restoration is a separate operation from backup configuration. Use the person's backup provider instructions for a harmless restore test; this review performed no restore. |

## Identity protection and everyday security

These are source anchors for use-time research, not maintained country playbooks or installed integrations. Provider availability, verification, representation rules, retention exceptions and deadlines must be checked for the person and exact request. No removal, enrollment, monitoring subscription or paid service was tested.

| Source checked 2026-10-31 | Supported scope / limit |
| --- | --- |
| [FTC people-search sites](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-people-search-sites-sell-your-information) | Opt-out processes vary; information can reappear after record changes or remain in associates' reports. Opting out does not erase government public records. Verification may be required. Supports a bounded recheck, not a promise to erase every copy. |
| [Google Results about you](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/12719076?hl=en) | Can find eligible personal information in Google Search and offer notifications/removal requests where available. Age, market and public-interest limits apply. Approval can remove a URL for all searches or only name-based queries; the source page remains. Requests on someone else's behalf use the appropriate detailed form, not an assumed entitlement through the assistant's account. |
| [California DROP: how it works](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/how-drop-works/) | California-resident service with broker-specific pending, deleted, exempted, opted-out and record-not-found outcomes. Ongoing matching does not cover all internet data; first-party, public and exempt data have limits. A not-found result is not proof of deletion. Users keep the private tracking ID themselves. Verify current processing schedule and eligibility at use time. |
| [California privacy rights FAQ](https://www.oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) and [CalPrivacy current laws and regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/) | Distinct deletion, sale/sharing opt-out and other rights have different scope and exceptions. A business may require signed authority and direct verification for an agent's request. Conversational permission does not establish legal representative status. Use the current regulator rules: the FAQ contains older regulatory-status wording, so do not reuse its statements about unfinished rulemaking. |
| [UK ICO erasure guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/your-right-to-get-your-data-deleted/) and [search results](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/internet-search-results/) | Erasure and search-result removal are conditional rights, with public-interest and retention exceptions. ICO flags this guidance as under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act. Recheck the governing guidance before giving deadlines or eligibility advice; do not apply UK rules worldwide. |
| [Argentina AAIP personal-data rights](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/derechos) and [current data-protection portal](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales) | Official starting points for access, correction, suppression and complaint routes. Confirm current procedure, applicability and exceptions for the particular holder and data; no universal deletion entitlement or recurring submission is inferred. |
| [HIBP FAQ](https://haveibeenpwned.com/FAQs) and [API documentation](https://haveibeenpwned.com/scalar/) | Known breach data has verification/sensitivity limits; a historical match does not prove current takeover, and absence is not assurance. API endpoints have authentication, subscription, ownership-verification and usage requirements. This package supplies no API client, paid access or comprehensive dark-web monitor. Never acquire leaked credentials or collect the user's password to perform a lookup. |
| [CFPB identity-monitoring overview](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-identity-monitoring-or-identity-theft-service-en-1369/) | Commercial services vary in signals, recovery help, fees and insurance. Monitoring is distinct from prevention and from local credit restrictions. The package supplies neither insurance nor a monitored credit feed; verify provider scope and current local options before recommending enrollment. |
| [FTC tech-support scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-spot-avoid-and-report-tech-support-scams) and [refund/recovery scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/refund-and-recovery-scams) | Unsolicited remote-access offers, fake warnings and unexpected paid recovery offers can be scams. Independently reach the provider through a known official channel. Official reporting and containment do not guarantee recovered funds. |
| [FTC website/app tracking](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-websites-and-apps-collect-and-use-your-information) | Review unnecessary permissions and supported privacy/advertising controls. Private browsing does not hide activity from websites; ad blocking does not promise malware protection or stop all tracking. No blanket browser reset or extension installation follows from this guidance. |
| [Android app permissions](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9431959?hl=en) and [Apple privacy/location controls](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102515) | Per-app and permission-type controls can limit access; availability and effects depend on OS/app. Location restrictions may affect useful features. Verify the relevant control and needed functionality, rather than disabling all permissions at once. |
| [UK NCSC family backup guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/always-back-up-your-most-important-data) | Protect the cloud account, keep important copies separately, check recovery and disconnect removable backup media when unused to reduce malware exposure. Choose a feasible backup method and verify current provider instructions; this is not a mandatory new product or fixed storage quota. |

CISA's ransomware and mobile-app pages returned access errors during this review; they were not treated as verified evidence. The accessible NCSC, FTC and platform sources above support the corresponding guidance. This records a retrieval gap, not a claim that CISA guidance is unavailable to users.

## Source-method lesson: official pages can outlive a service

The earlier country review found a reachable official enrollment page whose underlying rules had been repealed; [AAIP Resolución 180/2025](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/resoluci%C3%B3n-180-2025-418054/texto) is the primary-source example checked on 2026-10-24. That failure mode is retained here; the retired procedure, contact details and eligibility rules are no longer a maintained playbook. At use time, inspect the responsible authority's current service and governing updates before recommending enrollment. A repeal alone does not establish a replacement service or new duty.

## Verify at use time

Use [jurisdiction discovery](jurisdictions.md) for every local route. Carrier lock names, bank/token menus, foreign-address identity checks, device support and government protections vary. Official starting pages do not prove individual eligibility or successful configuration. Exact steps need a fresh source and user-side confirmation. Avoid broad negative claims about a country's protections without comprehensive current evidence.

Some official pages may block automated retrieval. This leaves a verification gap; it does not prove a service is unavailable. State the gap and use a verified official support route where available. Cadences here are design choices. Scheduler support, authorized access, execution and delivery each need their own evidence.
