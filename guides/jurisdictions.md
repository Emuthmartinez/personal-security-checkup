# Research the jurisdiction for this action

Use this guide for every country. Research only the next useful protection or recovery route; do not assemble a country dossier before helping with email recovery or urgent containment.

## Resolve only the context that changes the route

Use what the person already said. If needed, ask one short question at a time:

- Where do they live now? Ask their present location separately only when local help or in-person service depends on it.
- Which country serves or regulates the affected account, carrier or service, or issued the affected identity document? A provider's brand or language alone may not establish this.
- Are there relevant ties elsewhere, such as an identity document, financial account or recovery phone? Ask for country and type of tie, never its number or a document copy.

Residence, citizenship, account country and tax obligations are different facts. Do not infer one from another, infer tax-filing duties, or collect all of them by default. For multiple jurisdictions, research each affected route and deduplicate shared recovery steps. Confirm foreign-address, overseas-contact and remote-verification support when relevant. Never suggest false addresses or substitute identity details to pass eligibility checks.

## Establish the current official route

Find the responsible provider, regulator, public authority or official registry for the specific need. Verify authority through the institution's own navigation, cross-links and current remit; a search result, domain suffix or reachable page alone is insufficient. Follow official links when a service uses another host.

Check publication/effective dates, current service availability, amendments, repeal and superseding guidance. A live official enrollment page can describe a retired service. Resolve conflicts using the current responsible authority and applicable rule; do not infer replacement duties from a repeal. If you cannot establish currency or eligibility, say what is unresolved and offer the authority's verified support route. Do not invent a local equivalent, contact detail, identifier format or exact menu path. “Not verified” does not mean “does not exist.” If local sources are inaccessible, keep that gap explicit and continue useful country-neutral help.

| Need | Verify for this person and action |
| --- | --- |
| Credit or identity protection | Available record access/correction, fraud alerts or restrictions; eligibility, coverage, cost, lifting and recovery. Do not assume another country's freeze exists here or that monitoring is equivalent. |
| Footprint/removal request | Source/controller and applicable privacy authority; deletion, suppression and search-removal scope, exceptions, authorized-agent proof, private identity verification, response/appeal windows and cross-border support. An AI assistant is not automatically a legally authorized representative. |
| Lost document | Current issuing authority, replacement/reporting route, location and any relevant age/guardian rules. User handles documents privately. |
| Government login | Supported protection and recovery, delegated access and consequences of changes. No inferred legal or tax duties. |
| Phone and cross-border recovery | Carrier-supported SIM/transfer protection; accepted recovery methods, overseas reachability and continuity before canceling a line. Device SIM PIN and remote erasure are separate. |
| Money fraud | Provider's immediate containment route first; current complaint escalation and crime-reporting routes. A waiting period for escalation must not delay urgent contact. |

Explain the official destination, scope and limitations, who acts, non-secret verification and stop point using the core one-action format. An unfamiliar record may be an error or fraud; an empty record does not establish safety. Access rights or renewal rules do not automatically justify recurring submissions.

## Keep enough evidence to resume

For a consequential local recommendation, provide the official source link, date checked, applicable jurisdiction/service, supported claim, eligibility limits and unresolved questions. With permission, retain only that minimal context in the private note. Reverify before a later action when rules or circumstances may have changed. Never publish the person's local findings in this repository.

The old files under `countries/` are compatibility redirects. They do not establish current routes or eligibility. [Source-method notes](sources.md) explain why official status alone is insufficient.
